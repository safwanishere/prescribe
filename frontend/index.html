Clinical notes, finally usable.

Most consultation notes live on paper or in scans that nobody can search. Prescribe makes them readable by software without changing how doctors write.

Reads handwriting and print

OCR trained to handle the abbreviations, shorthand and messy handwriting common in clinical notes.

Understands medical language

NLP identifies symptoms, diagnoses, drugs, doses and follow-up instructions, then labels each one.

Searchable and reviewable

Find any patient visit by condition or medication, and check every extracted field against the original note.
